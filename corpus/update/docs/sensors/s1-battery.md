# Sense S1 battery life and replacement

## Battery life

The Sense S1 runs on two AA lithium batteries. With sensor firmware 3.2 or later, expected battery life is 24 months at the default reporting interval of 5 minutes. A reporting interval of 1 minute reduces battery life to about 8 months.

## Low battery warning

Pellwood Cloud raises a low battery warning when the battery level of a sensor drops below 15 percent.

## Replacing batteries

Twist the base of the sensor anticlockwise to open it. Replace both batteries at the same time. Do not use alkaline or rechargeable batteries, because their voltage curve makes the battery level reading inaccurate.
