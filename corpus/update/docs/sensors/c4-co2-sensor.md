# Sense C4 carbon dioxide sensor

## What it measures

The Sense C4 measures carbon dioxide concentration from 400 to 5000 parts per million, together with air temperature. It uses the same radio as the Sense S1 and pairs with a Hub H2 in the same way.

## Power

The Sense C4 is powered by a USB-C adapter and has no batteries. It needs a warm-up time of 3 minutes after power is connected before carbon dioxide readings are valid.
