# Sense S1 placement guidelines

## Height and position

Mount the Sense S1 at crop canopy height, away from direct sunlight, heaters and misting nozzles. Use the supplied radiation shield in glasshouses with strong direct light.

## Density

Use at least one sensor per 200 square metres of growing area, and at least two sensors per zone so that a single failed sensor does not leave a zone unmonitored.

## Range

Keep each sensor within 300 metres of the hub or within 100 metres of another sensor that can act as a mesh hop.
