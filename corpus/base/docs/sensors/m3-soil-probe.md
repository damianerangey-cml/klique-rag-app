# Probe M3 soil moisture probe

## What it measures

The Probe M3 measures volumetric water content of soil or substrate from 0 to 60 percent, and substrate temperature. It has a 2 metre cable and plugs into the accessory port of a Sense S1. It has no battery of its own.

## Installation

Insert the probe vertically until the two steel pins are fully covered. In rockwool slabs insert it horizontally, halfway up the slab. Allow 24 hours for readings to settle after installation.
