# Sense S1 calibration

## Factory calibration

Every Sense S1 is calibrated at the factory. Temperature accuracy is plus or minus 0.2 degrees Celsius and relative humidity accuracy is plus or minus 2 percent.

## Recalibration schedule

Recalibrate humidity every 24 months, or sooner if the sensor has been exposed to condensation for long periods. Temperature does not need recalibration.

## Offset adjustment

A manual offset of up to 1.5 degrees or 5 percent humidity can be applied per sensor in Pellwood Cloud under Sensor settings, Calibration.
