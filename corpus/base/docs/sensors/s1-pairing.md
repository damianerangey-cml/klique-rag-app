# Pairing a Sense S1 with a hub

## Pairing steps

1. In the mobile app open the zone and choose Add sensor.
2. Pull the battery tab from the sensor. The sensor light blinks green.
3. Hold the sensor within 2 metres of the hub until the app shows it as paired.

Pairing takes about 30 seconds. A sensor can be paired with only one hub at a time.

## Moving a sensor to another hub

Remove the sensor from its zone in Pellwood Cloud, then press the pairing button inside the battery compartment for 5 seconds and pair it again.
