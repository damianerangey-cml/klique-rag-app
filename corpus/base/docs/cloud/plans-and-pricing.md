# Plans and pricing

## Plans

Pellwood Cloud has two plans. The Standard plan costs 29 euros per site per month and includes up to 50 sensors. The Enterprise plan costs 99 euros per site per month, has no sensor limit, and adds single sign-on, scheduled export and longer data retention.

## Billing

Plans are billed annually in advance. A site can be upgraded from Standard to Enterprise at any time, and the price difference is charged pro rata.
