# Hub H2 factory reset

## When to reset

A factory reset removes the Wi-Fi configuration and all paired sensors from the hub. Use it before moving a hub to a different site or handing it to another owner.

## How to reset

Hold the recessed reset button on the back of the hub for 10 seconds with a paper clip, until the status light blinks blue. Readings already uploaded to Pellwood Cloud are not deleted by a reset.
