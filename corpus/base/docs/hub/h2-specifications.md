# Hub H2 specifications

## Capacity and radio

One Hub H2 supports up to 120 paired sensors. The sensor radio operates in the 868 MHz band in Europe and the 915 MHz band in North America. Open-air range to a sensor is about 300 metres.

## Power and environment

The hub draws 4 W from the supplied 12 V adapter. It includes a backup battery that keeps it running for 6 hours during a power cut. The operating temperature range is -10 to 50 degrees Celsius. The enclosure is rated IP54.
